Why it is small

A 10-minute battery holds about 0.7% of a day's energy. Even cycled every day it can only move that sliver into cleaner hours, and round-trip losses eat part of the gain. Compare this with the Facility headroom tab, where moving work itself does far more.

Average or marginal carbon?

Clean hours can be hot hours

Grid draw is IT load times PUE, and cooling works harder when it is hot outside. On solar grids the cleanest hours are also the hottest, so a scheduler that watches only carbon claims more than it saves. Real 2022 weather at one data-centre hub per region; 24 h slack.
